Residences

The Maharaja Suite

Our most private residence — a four-poster chamber, rose-petal bath, and a terrace that opens directly onto the lake. Designed for those who prefer the palace to feel like a single, uninterrupted thought.

The Moon Pavilion

A luminous suite oriented to the eastern water. Soft textiles, hand-painted ceilings, and a sitting room where dawn arrives first. Ideal for longer stays and quiet mornings.

The Courtyard Chamber

Overlooking the inner gardens and elephant fountains. Warm sandstone, brass lanterns, and the gentle sound of water through the day. A refined balance of intimacy and openness.

The Lake View Room

Floor-to-ceiling views across the water, a deep soaking tub, and restrained royal detailing. Perfect for guests who wish to wake with the reflection of the palace on the lake.

The Heritage Duplex

Two levels linked by a carved stair — living space below, sleeping chamber above. For families or companions who value both togetherness and privacy within the palace walls.

The Garden Pavilion

A secluded ground-level residence opening onto private lawn and pool edge. Peacocks often pass at dusk. Soft, grounded, and deeply restful.
